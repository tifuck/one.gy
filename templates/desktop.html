What it does

  • The website in its own window

    The app is ONE.GY in its own window, with Files, Notes{% if allow_music %} and Music{% endif %} as you know them. Once you sign in, the Sync button in the navigation sets up folder sync on this computer.

  • Live folder sync

    Pick a folder on your computer and one in your drive. Changes on either side reach the other as they happen.

  • End-to-end encrypted

    Files and their names are encrypted on your computer with the same keys as the website. The server only stores ciphertext.

  • As many syncs as you need

    Run several folder pairs at once, each with its own settings.

  • Two-way, backup or mirror

    Two-way keeps both sides the same. Backup only uploads, and the copy in your drive is read-only. Mirror keeps this computer matching a folder in your drive.

  • Works with self-hosted servers

    Sign in to this server or any other one.gy server by entering its address.
