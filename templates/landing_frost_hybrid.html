Encrypted before it leaves your device.

Every step that touches your data runs in your browser or the CLI. Our servers only ever handle the sealed result.

  1. 1

    Encrypt locally

    Your browser derives keys from your password and seals each file and filename with AES-256-GCM.

    report.pdf ↓ a7f3 09c2 e81b 4d6f 2c90 …
  2. 2

    Store ciphertext

    We receive encrypted bytes and a SHA-256 hash of your auth token. Replicas are checksum-verified on every node.

    PUT /upload 9f3a 0c7e b21d … c1e0 ✓ checksum verified
  3. 3

    Share with the key in the link

    The key rides after the #. Browsers never send that part to a server, so only the recipient can decrypt.

    {{ domain }}/f/a1b2c3#passphrase=… fragment stays in the browser
