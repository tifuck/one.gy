The method

Your password is stretched with PBKDF2 (600k iterations) and split into two independent values in your browser.

Half one becomes an auth token; we store only its SHA-256 hash.

Half two unwraps your master key, which never leaves the browser and derives per-file keys, an RSA-4096 sharing key and HKDF link secrets.

What reaches our servers is ciphertext and hashes. Nothing stored can decrypt your files.
